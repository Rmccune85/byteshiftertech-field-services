Why ByteShifter

Field service built from real IT support work.

ByteShifter Technologies is focused on the part of IT that still requires someone to physically show up: deploying equipment, replacing hardware, tracing connections, troubleshooting devices, working with remote engineers, and documenting the result.

The company is led by Ron McCune, an IT support professional with hands-on experience supporting enterprise users and technology environments. The approach is intentionally practical: follow the scope, protect the customer’s environment, communicate clearly, verify the work, and escalate what belongs with a specialized engineer.

Learn more about Ron's IT background, skills, coursework, and projects at ronmccune.com .

ByteShifter is not positioned as an all-in-one managed service provider. The focus is on dependable on-site execution and becoming a trusted regional resource for businesses and technical teams.
