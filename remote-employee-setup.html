What’s Included

Hands-on support with a clear scope.

  • Company laptop or desktop setup at the employee workspace
  • Docking station and multi-monitor connection
  • Keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam, and approved peripherals
  • Printer or scanner setup when included in scope
  • Ethernet / Wi-Fi connectivity checks and basic local troubleshooting
